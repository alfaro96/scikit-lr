Version 0.3.0
=============

**October 2026**

* scikit-lr requires Python 3.13 or later, and is built with meson-python.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

* The public API has type hints, which type checkers find in the installed package
  (:pep:`561`).
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

* There are wheels for Linux (x86_64 and aarch64), macOS (x86_64 and arm64)
  and Windows (AMD64 and ARM64).
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

* The extension modules extend the internals of scikit-learn, so scikit-lr is
  built against a single minor release of scikit-learn, 1.9, and importing
  :mod:`sklr` with another one raises an :class:`ImportError`.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

* Every ranker works with the tools of scikit-learn that take an estimator, such
  as :class:`~sklearn.pipeline.Pipeline`,
  :class:`~sklearn.model_selection.GridSearchCV` and
  :func:`~sklearn.model_selection.cross_validate`, and accepts pandas data frames.
  The trees and the dummy rankers take sample weights in ``fit`` and every ranker
  takes them in ``score``, which metadata routing passes through pipelines and
  model selection tools, as described in :ref:`sklearn_integration`.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

:mod:`sklr`
-----------

* |Feature| :func:`show_versions` prints the versions of scikit-lr, of the scikit-learn that
  it was built against and of the dependencies, to include them in bug reports.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

:mod:`sklr.base`
----------------

* |Feature| :class:`base.LabelRankerMixin` and :class:`base.PartialLabelRankerMixin` set the
  tags of the label rankers and of the partial label rankers, and their ``score``
  method computes :func:`metrics.kendall_tau_score` and :func:`metrics.tau_x_score`
  on the predictions of the ranker.
  :func:`base.is_label_ranker` and :func:`base.is_partial_label_ranker` tell them
  apart from other estimators.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

:mod:`sklr.dummy`
-----------------

* |MajorFeature| :class:`dummy.DummyLabelRanker` and :class:`dummy.DummyPartialLabelRanker`
  predict, for every sample, the consensus of the possibly incomplete training
  rankings or a given ranking, as baselines to compare with other rankers.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

:mod:`sklr.metrics`
-------------------

* |Feature| :func:`metrics.kendall_tau_score` and :func:`metrics.kendall_distance` compare
  label rankings, and :func:`metrics.tau_x_score` compares partial label rankings,
  averaging over the samples with optional sample weights.
  :func:`metrics.get_scorer` gets their scorers by name, to pass them to the
  ``scoring`` parameter of the model selection tools of scikit-learn, and
  :func:`metrics.get_scorer_names` lists the names.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

:mod:`sklr.neighbors`
---------------------

* |MajorFeature| :class:`neighbors.KNeighborsLabelRanker` predicts the ranking of each sample from
  the possibly incomplete rankings of its nearest neighbors, with the Mallows or the
  Plackett-Luce model, and weights the neighbors equally, by the inverse of their
  distance or by the rule of Dudani.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

* |MajorFeature| :class:`neighbors.KNeighborsPartialLabelRanker` predicts the bucket order of each
  sample from the possibly incomplete partial rankings of its nearest neighbors,
  with the Bucket Pivot Algorithm, and weights the neighbors equally, by the inverse
  of their distance or by the rule of Dudani.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

* |Efficiency| :class:`neighbors.KNeighborsLabelRanker` and
  :class:`neighbors.KNeighborsPartialLabelRanker` estimate the rankings of the
  queries in parallel, with the number of threads set by ``n_jobs``, which also
  sets those of the neighbor search.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

:mod:`sklr.tree`
----------------

* |MajorFeature| :class:`tree.DecisionTreeLabelRanker` grows a decision tree that fits the Mallows
  model to the possibly incomplete rankings of each node and predicts the center ranking
  of each leaf, with the parameters, the sample weights, the missing values in the
  features, the pruning and the export with :func:`sklearn.tree.export_graphviz` of the
  trees of scikit-learn.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

* |MajorFeature| :class:`tree.DecisionTreePartialLabelRanker` grows a decision tree from possibly
  incomplete partial rankings, with the disagreements, distance, entropy and Gini
  impurities of the pairs of labels, and predicts the bucket order of each leaf with the
  Bucket Pivot Algorithm, with the parameters, the sample weights, the missing values in
  the features, the pruning and the export with :func:`sklearn.tree.export_graphviz` of
  the trees of scikit-learn.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

:mod:`sklr.utils`
-----------------

* |Feature| :func:`utils.check_ranking` validates the rankings of a target and converts them
  to a ``float64`` array, rejecting rankings with ties and incomplete rankings
  unless they are allowed.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`

* |Feature| :func:`utils.type_of_ranking` determines whether a target holds label rankings,
  partial label rankings or does not encode rankings, following the representation
  described in :ref:`ranking_representation`.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`99`
