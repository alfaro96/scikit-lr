Version 0.4.0
=============

**In development**

:mod:`sklr.metrics`
-------------------

* |Feature| :func:`metrics.spearman_rho_score` computes Spearman's :math:`\rho` between label
  rankings, and the ``"spearman_rho"`` scorer of :func:`metrics.get_scorer` brings
  it to the model selection tools.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`109`

:mod:`sklr.reduction`
---------------------

* |MajorFeature| :class:`reduction.LabelwiseLabelRanker` fits a probabilistic classifier to the
  positions of each label, by default an ordinal logistic regression, and predicts
  the ranking that minimizes the expected absolute or squared error of the
  positions. It learns from incomplete rankings with the default classifier or any
  other with a ``fit_likelihood`` method.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`107`

* |MajorFeature| :class:`reduction.PairwiseLabelRanker` fits a probabilistic classifier to each
  pair of labels, by default a logistic regression, and ranks the labels by the sum
  of the probabilities that each one is ahead of the others, which minimizes the
  expected squared error of the positions when the probabilities are right. It
  learns from incomplete rankings, fitting the classifier of each pair to the
  rankings that rank both of its labels.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`. :pr:`109`
