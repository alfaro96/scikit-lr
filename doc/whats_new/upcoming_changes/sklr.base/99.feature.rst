* :class:`base.LabelRankerMixin` and :class:`base.PartialLabelRankerMixin` set the
  tags of the label rankers and of the partial label rankers, and their ``score``
  method computes :func:`metrics.kendall_tau_score` and
  :func:`metrics.kendall_tau_x_score` on the predictions of the ranker.
  :func:`base.is_label_ranker` and :func:`base.is_partial_label_ranker` tell them
  apart from other estimators.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`.
