* :class:`tree.DecisionTreePartialLabelRanker` grows a decision tree from possibly
  incomplete partial rankings, with the disagreements, distance, entropy and Gini
  impurities of the pairs of labels, and predicts the bucket order of each leaf with the
  Bucket Pivot Algorithm, with the parameters, the sample weights, the missing values in
  the features, the pruning and the export with :func:`sklearn.tree.export_graphviz` of
  the trees of scikit-learn.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`.
