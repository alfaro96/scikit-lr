* Every ranker works with the tools of scikit-learn that take an estimator, such
  as :class:`~sklearn.pipeline.Pipeline`,
  :class:`~sklearn.model_selection.GridSearchCV` and
  :func:`~sklearn.model_selection.cross_validate`, and accepts pandas data frames.
  The trees and the dummy rankers take sample weights in ``fit`` and every ranker
  takes them in ``score``, which metadata routing passes through pipelines and
  model selection tools, as described in :ref:`sklearn_integration`.
  By :user:`Juan Carlos Alfaro Jiménez <alfaro96>`.
