Getting started
===============

The estimators of scikit-lr follow the scikit-learn API: they are fitted to
the training data with ``fit`` and make predictions for new samples with
``predict``, so the scikit-learn :doc:`getting started guide
<sklearn:getting_started>` also applies to them. The :ref:`user guide
<user_guide>` describes the label ranking problems that they solve and how
the rankings are represented.
